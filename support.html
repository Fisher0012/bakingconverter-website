FAQ

Does this app require an internet connection?

No. Bake Ratio works entirely offline. No account, no login, no data connection needed.

Which units are supported?

Bake Ratio supports grams (g), ounces (oz), cups, tablespoons (tbsp), and teaspoons (tsp).

How do I scale a recipe?

Enter your original serving count and your target serving count. All ingredient quantities will update automatically.

Can I save my own recipes?

Yes. You can favorite and save custom recipes for quick access. All data is stored locally on your device.

Is there any advertising in the app?

No. Bake Ratio is completely ad-free and does not track any user data.
