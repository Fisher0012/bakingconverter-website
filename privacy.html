Bake Ratio does not collect any data.
No personal information, no usage analytics, no third-party tracking. Everything stays on your device.

1. Data Collection

Bake Ratio does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. The app runs entirely offline and does not communicate with any server.

2. Local Storage

Your recipes and preferences are stored locally on your device using iOS UserDefaults. This data never leaves your device and is not accessible to us or any third party.

3. Third-Party Services

Bake Ratio does not integrate any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services.

4. Children's Privacy

Since we do not collect any data, there are no special provisions needed for children's privacy. The app is safe for users of all ages.

5. Changes to This Policy

If we ever update this policy, changes will be posted on this page with a revised date.
